## Coverage honesty

- Government, private-provider and public-access-venue semantics stay separate in the `ownership_semantics` field; a mixed source is not a government census.
- Facility, site, building, unit, project, asset and polygon are distinct in the register notes; a project list is not a facility list.
- Personal, security-sensitive and critical-infrastructure fields are flagged for review before publication.

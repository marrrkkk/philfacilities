# 10 — Acquisition Request Templates

These templates are starting points. Adapt the recipient, local context, categories, data fields, and legal references to the specific agency/LGU. Do not send requests using invented personal details, and do not request personal/sensitive information that is not needed.

A request is one route to clarify or obtain data. It is not a prerequisite when an exact release is already verified and publicly available with no explicit prohibition: that basis can be recorded under the root [data inclusion and publication policy](../../docs/14-DATA-INCLUSION-AND-PUBLICATION-POLICY.md). A request or clarification is still required where an access control, agreement, FOI condition, or genuinely ambiguous restriction applies.

## A. LGU periodic inventory of infrastructure and community facilities

**Subject:** Request for existing inventory of public/community facilities and qualifying public-service venues

Dear [Local Chief Executive / Department / FOI Officer],

I am requesting a copy of the latest existing inventory of public facilities, community facilities, and other qualifying public-service venues maintained or recorded by [LGU name], for use in a public-interest civic technology project called PhilFacilities. The project organizes publicly releasable facility information into a searchable national directory and map, with source attribution and data-quality information.

If available, please provide the latest inventory and any previous versions in CSV, XLSX, GeoJSON, shapefile, GeoPackage, or another existing machine-readable format. We are not asking the LGU to create new data if an existing inventory is already maintained.

Please include existing records for the categories held by your office, which may include:

- barangay halls and barangay centres;
- city/municipal/provincial halls and satellite government offices;
- multipurpose halls, civic centres, covered courts, gymnasiums and sports complexes;
- public parks, plazas, playgrounds and open spaces;
- public markets, slaughterhouses and other LGU economic facilities;
- evacuation centres, DRRM offices, command centres and emergency facilities;
- public libraries, cultural/community centres and social welfare facilities;
- public health, education and other public-service facilities;
- other infrastructure and community facilities included in the LGU's inventory.

For each record, please provide existing fields where available:

1. official facility name and existing facility/asset ID;
2. facility type/category;
3. barangay, city/municipality, province and street/address;
4. latitude/longitude or GIS geometry, including coordinate reference system and collection method if known;
5. owner, operator and managing office/entity;
6. source-reported operating/lifecycle status and the date that status applies;
7. facility function/designation, including multi-use functions;
8. capacity and unit, if recorded;
9. establishment/opening date or asset registration date, if recorded;
10. inventory reference date and responsible office;
11. public-use restrictions or fields that cannot be disclosed.

Section 524 of Republic Act No. 7160 refers to periodic LGU inventories of infrastructure and other community facilities. This request is for an existing releasable inventory; it does not assume that the LGU has a centralized database or that all fields listed above are maintained.

Please identify any data-use, attribution, confidentiality, or redistribution conditions that apply to the records. If some fields are restricted, please provide a public/redacted version where possible and identify which fields were withheld.

Thank you.

## B. Barangay halls, multipurpose halls, covered courts, parks and plazas

**Subject:** Request for existing barangay and community facility inventory for [LGU]

Please provide the latest existing list or inventory of barangay halls, multipurpose halls, civic/community centres, covered courts, public gymnasiums, parks, plazas, open spaces and playgrounds within [LGU].

For each facility, please provide any existing name/ID, barangay, address, coordinates or GIS geometry, managing office, owner/operator, functions/designations, capacity and current source-reported status. Please distinguish a facility's regular function from any additional designation (for example, a covered court also designated as an evacuation centre).

If the inventory is maintained by different offices or barangays, please identify the responsible offices and whether the LGU has a consolidated list. Please provide the source date and applicable terms.

## C. Public markets, slaughterhouses and LGU economic facilities

**Subject:** Request for official inventory of LGU-operated public markets and related facilities

Please provide the latest existing inventory of public markets, market buildings/complexes, slaughterhouses/abattoirs and other publicly operated local economic facilities within [LGU].

Where maintained, please include the facility ID/name, address, barangay, coordinates/GIS geometry, owner/operator, number of market buildings or stalls, facility type, operating status, and the source date. Do not include personal stallholder or vendor information unless it is explicitly public and necessary; facility-level information is sufficient.

Please identify whether private markets or privately operated public markets are included. Distinguish owner, operator, LGU regulator/manager, public-access conditions and the source basis for each listing.

## D. Evacuation centres and DRRM facilities

**Subject:** Request for current inventory of designated evacuation centres and DRRM facilities

Please provide the latest existing inventory of officially designated evacuation centres and other DRRM facilities within [LGU/coverage area]. This may include evacuation centres, emergency operations/command centres, DRRM warehouses, rescue stations, and other designated response facilities.

For each site, please provide the facility name/ID, address, barangay, coordinates and coordinate method, designated function, source-reported capacity and unit, managing office, public accessibility, status, date of last validation, and source/reference date, where available.

Please distinguish designated evacuation centres from schools, halls, courts or other buildings that may only be used temporarily during an event. Do not provide sensitive security layouts, equipment locations, staff assignments, or non-public operational information.

## E. BFP fire stations and fire-response facilities

**Subject:** Request for the latest official list of BFP fire stations and publicly listed fire-response facilities

Please provide the latest existing national/regional/provincial list of BFP fire stations and publicly listed fire-response facilities, preferably in CSV/XLSX or GIS format. Please include station name/ID, station type, parent office, address, city/municipality, province, region, public contact channel, coordinates and collection method if held, and source date.

Please identify whether volunteer fire brigades are included, and if not, whether a separate public registry is held by BFP, LGUs, or another authority. Please identify any location/security fields that should not be disclosed.

## F. PNP police station/precinct directory

**Subject:** Request for current official directory of public-facing police stations and precincts

Please provide the latest official list of public-facing PNP police stations/precincts and their official contact/service addresses within [coverage area], preferably in CSV/XLSX or GIS format. Please include station/precinct name or ID, district/provincial/regional office, public address, city/municipality, region, official public contact channel, and coordinates if publicly releasable.

This request is limited to public-facing facility directory information. It does not request patrol patterns, personnel rosters, security plans, surveillance information, restricted access details, operational vulnerabilities, or sensitive deployment data.

## G. National-government-owned buildings

**Subject:** Request for releasable inventory of national government-owned buildings within [coverage]

Please provide the latest existing inventory of national government-owned buildings under your agency's custody/management or within [coverage], including any existing inventory template or GIS file. Where publicly releasable, include building/asset ID, building name/type, agency/user, address/location, coordinates or geotagged location, floor/lot area, storeys, status/condition, year/period of inventory, and responsible office.

Please identify any fields or locations withheld for security, operational, or other lawful reasons. A redacted public version is acceptable.

## H. Follow-up questions when the agency says it has no central dataset

If no consolidated list is held, ask:

1. Which office or LGU department maintains the records?
2. Does each regional/provincial/city/municipal office maintain a separate inventory?
3. Is there an existing template, memorandum, reporting form, or inventory standard?
4. Is any portion available through an official webpage, GIS portal, or downloadable file?
5. Can the agency provide a list of custodians or offices from which local data may be requested?
6. Can a sample redacted file/schema be provided to understand the structure?

Log negative responses and referrals in the source register. Do not infer that no data exists merely because the central office does not hold it.


## I. Official directory of private/public-service providers

**Subject:** Request for existing public directory or registry of licensed, accredited, registered or contracted facility-level service providers

Dear [Agency / FOI Officer],

I am requesting the latest existing facility-level directory or dataset maintained by [agency] for providers of [healthcare / education / social welfare / utilities / transport / other service] within [coverage]. This request is for physical facility/service-location records, not only company or organization-level registration records.

If available, please provide an existing CSV, XLSX, JSON, XML, PDF, GeoJSON, shapefile or other machine-readable release, including the source publication/reference date and any prior versions. Please identify existing fields such as facility/provider ID, facility name/type, site/campus/branch address, geographic fields/coordinates and method, public contact channel, registration/licence/accreditation type and scope, status, validity/effective dates, and owner/operator where held.

Please distinguish private ownership from government operation, regulation, accreditation, funding, contracting, designation or hosting. If an agency lists a provider but does not know its legal owner/operator, please indicate that rather than infer it.

Please identify the applicable terms for access, storage, transformation, public redistribution and attribution. If no formal terms document exists, please confirm whether any explicit restriction applies to reusing factual facility fields. If some attributes cannot be redistributed, please identify those fields and whether a redacted/public version is available. We do not request personal beneficiary, patient, student, employee or resident information.

Thank you.
